About

A bit about me

I'm a student with a quiet obsession for how things work — from the software running behind a good product to the small design decisions that make it feel effortless. Most of what I know, I learned by taking something apart and trying to put it back together, usually better than before.

I care about clarity: in code, in writing, and in the things I build. Lately that curiosity has been split between technology and creation — building small projects, learning new tools, and occasionally writing down what I find along the way.

This site is a small, honest snapshot of that — a place to keep track of what I'm making and what I'm thinking about.
